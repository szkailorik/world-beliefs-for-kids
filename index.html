给 7-8 岁小朋友 For ages 7-8

我怎样看世界

How I See the World

世界大体安全，也很精彩。我们可以好奇、可以犯错、可以选择， 也要记得边界和后果。

The world is mostly safe and full of wonder. We can be curious, make mistakes, make choices, and remember boundaries and consequences.

安全

Safe

遇到困难时，我能找可靠的人帮忙。

When things are hard, I can ask reliable people for help.

精彩

Wonder

世界里有很多还没发现的有趣东西。

The world has many interesting things I have not found yet.

有回应

Responsive

我认真观察、提问和行动，世界会给我回声。

When I observe, ask, and act, the world answers back.
